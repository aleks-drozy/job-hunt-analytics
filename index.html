This page is descriptive, not inferential. At n = 50 applications, 65 operations and 19 days of coverage, nothing here supports a significance test, a confidence interval, or a causal claim - a single person's one-month sample doesn't carry that kind of weight. Every figure below comes from the committed, anonymised CSVs in results/, cell for cell.

§2 Funnel

Of 50 tracked applications: 43 submitted, 5 closed by the employer before ever being submitted, 1 skipped, and 1 reached an outcome the export couldn't classify cleanly. Of the 43 submitted: 15 rejected, 28 with no response yet. ‘Employer closed’ conflates a posting withdrawn by the employer with one filled by someone else - the export doesn't distinguish the two.

§3 Time to rejection

10 of 15 rejections had both an applied date and a status date and could be timed; 5 are excluded from the chart for missing one of those dates - excluded, not dropped: they are still counted in the 15 above. Timed rejections ranged 0-5 days.

§4 Channels

Applications sorted into 6 channels: other 22, company_portal 8, linkedin 8, indeed 5, workday 4, jooble 3. LinkedIn shows a 0.0% rejection rate across 8 submitted applications (0 of 8 rejected). That is not a good result: nothing came back at all. Workday, by contrast, shows 3 of 4 rejected.

§5 Tiers

Entry: 3 of 11 rejected. Stretch: 1 of 6 rejected. Unspecified: 11 of 33 rejected - unspecified carries most of the volume because tier is derived from role titles, and most role titles don't state a level.

§6 Operations

65 operations across 6 categories: 41 closed, 22 open, 2 snoozed. Of the closed ones, 24 carry a machine-readable close date (17 do not); those that do took 0-6 days.

§7 Finance

Buffer moved from 66.0% of target on 2026-07-10 to 88.0% on 2026-07-24, with income-change markers on 2 distinct dates. No monetary amount exists anywhere in this repository, by design - only the percentage of a private target.

§8 What this cannot tell you

  • The sample is small and covers one month, one person, and one job market - none of this generalises.
  • Several of the rates above are computed over small denominators (single digits up to the thirties); a couple of applications going differently would move a percentage a lot.
  • ‘No response yet’ is censored at the export date, not a confirmed non-response - some of it will still convert.
  • Tier (entry/stretch) is inferred from role titles, not self-reported, and most titles don't state a level.
  • ‘Closed’ in the funnel conflates postings withdrawn by the employer with postings filled by someone else.
  • Nothing on this page is causal. A channel or tier with a higher rejection rate is not shown to cause worse outcomes.
